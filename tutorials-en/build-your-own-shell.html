About

Build your own shell in C as a stage-graded project: a validate script takes you from a basic prompt loop to pipelines and builtins.

What you'll learn

  • A stage-based validator that grades your shell automatically
  • Reading commands, splitting tokens and forking
  • Implementing builtins like exit and cd without forking

Core idea

The project ships a ./validate harness with numbered stages - each stage adds features (echo, exit, cd, and later pipelines). Your mysh is exercised against a battery of scripted sessions.

Why it matters

Automated grading means instant feedback on shell internals like fork/exec and pipes - a rare, tight learning loop.
