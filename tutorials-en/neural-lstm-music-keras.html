About

Generate music with an LSTM in Keras: parse MIDI files with music21, window the note sequences, train, and sample new melodies.

What you'll learn

  • Parsing MIDI into a sequence of notes and chords with music21
  • Building windowed input/output pairs of length 100
  • An LSTM(512) model and sampling new sequences

Core idea

Trained music is a sequence problem: the LSTM sees the previous 100 symbols and predicts the next one. After training, a sampling loop feeds predictions back as input, generating entirely new melodies.
