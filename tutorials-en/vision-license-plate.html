About

Automatic license plate recognition in Python: segment characters from a car photo with scikit-image, then classify them with a small SVM.

What you'll learn

  • Preprocessing: grayscale, closing and masking operations
  • Finding character regions with label and regionprops
  • Training an SVM classifier on 20x20 character images

Core idea

The pipeline starts with a car image: it is gray-scaled, closed and region-labeled so candidate characters emerge as connected components. Each 20x20 crop becomes a feature vector for a scikit-learn SVM.
