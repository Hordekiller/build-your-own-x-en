About

A journey into the kernel's network layer: starting from a TAP device, you build Ethernet, ARP, IPv4, ICMP and UDP — your own TCP/IP stack in C.

What you'll learn

  • What TUN/TAP devices are and how the kernel exposes them
  • Parsing Ethernet frames and the ARP resolution protocol
  • IPv4 header handling and checksums
  • Sending UDP datagrams through your own stack

Core idea

The stack begins at the TAP interface: your program reads raw Ethernet frames from it, processes them layer by layer, and writes outgoing frames back. The receive loop is the heart of the whole stack.
