Write SMS-spam detector with Scikit-learn

About

Classify SMS as spam or ham with scikit-learn: convert messages to TF-IDF vectors, train a Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier and evaluate with accuracy plus confusion matrix - under 20 lines.

What you'll learn

  • TF-IDF: rare words weigh more than ubiquitous ones
  • Why MultinomialNB suits text classification
  • Precision/recall for spam - accuracy alone lies

Core idea

TF-IDF down-weights words common to all messages and boosts distinctive ones; NB is fast and naturally suited to word counts.

Pythonfrom sklearn.feature_extraction.text import TfidfVectorizer
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split
from sklearn.naive_bayes import MultinomialNB
from sklearn.metrics import accuracy_score, confusion_matrix

# df: columns 'text' and 'label' (spam/ham)
y = df['label'].map({'spam': 1, 'ham': 0})
vec = TfidfVectorizer(stop_words='english')
Xv = vec.fit_transform(df['text'])
X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(
    Xv, y, test_size=0.2, random_state=42)

model = MultinomialNB()
model.fit(X_train, y_train)
pred = model.predict(X_test)
print('accuracy:', accuracy_score(y_test, pred).round(3))
print(confusion_matrix(y_test, pred))

Why it matters

A classic NLP entry point that still works in production - and teaches why accuracy is not enough.
