About

The classic Princeton reference matcher: a recursive-descent regex engine in C with ^, $, ., * and character classes.

What you'll learn

  • The match/matchhere pattern for anchor handling
  • Star matching greedily with backtracking
  • Literals, dots and the end-of-string rule

Core idea

match() handles anchors at the start, then delegates to matchhere(). The star case tries the longest match first and backs off - the simplest form of backtracking.

{

Why it matters

This tiny C matcher is the ancestor of many real implementations - the cleanest reference for recursive regex matching.
