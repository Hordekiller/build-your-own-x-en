About

Build Linux from scratch: compile the entire toolchain, glibc and kernel yourself, then boot your own personal distribution.

What you'll learn

  • Preparing a partition and the essential tools
  • Building a temporary toolchain (GCC pass 1) into $LFS/tools
  • Cross-compiling glibc and the kernel, then chrooting into your system

Core idea

LFS's chapter 5 builds a temporary toolchain that is not yet linked against glibc. GCC's first pass is configured with --prefix=$LFS/tools and a handful of flags that keep it independent of the host.

bash# a summary of building GCC pass 1 (LFS chapter 5)
mkdir -v build && cd build
../configure --prefix=$LFS/tools \
    --with-local-prefix=$LFS/tools \
    --disable-nls --disable-shared --disable-libssp \
    --enable-languages=c,c++

Why it matters

There is no deeper literacy than having compiled every piece of your OS yourself — LFS is the definitive rite of passage for Linux engineers.
