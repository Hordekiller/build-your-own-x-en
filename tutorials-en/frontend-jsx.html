About

WTF is JSX? A tiny JSX renderer that shows exactly what JSX compiles to and why: createElement, virtual nodes, and a diff that repaints only what changed.

What you'll learn

  • What Babel turns JSX into: plain createElement calls
  • Building vnodes and rendering them to the DOM
  • Diffing old and new nodes with minimal DOM surgery
  • Thinking in elements, not markup

Why it matters

JSX stops being magic the minute you compile it by hand — and this tiny renderer hands you the mental model behind React's whole rendering pipeline.
