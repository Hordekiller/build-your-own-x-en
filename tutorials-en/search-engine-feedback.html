About

Search relevance through feedback: implement pseudo-relevance feedback on a vector-space search engine, re-weighting queries with the top results.

What you'll learn

  • Scoring documents as a weighted sum of features
  • Combining a base score with feedback signals
  • Using neighbors' positive feedback as a retrieval feature

Core idea

Relevance is scored as a weighted sum of feature functions. A feedback feature looks at the nearest previously-clicked query; its similarity times the feedback ratio inflates the score of near-miss documents.
