About

A DIY guide to build your own React: Didact walks through every React 16 feature — elements, rendering, component classes, reconciliation, fibers and hooks — in readable JavaScript.

What you'll learn

  • The React architecture distilled into a series of small steps
  • Reconciliation: comparing fibers and committing minimal patches
  • Implementing useState on top of the fiber tree
  • When hooks really are: fields on a fiber
