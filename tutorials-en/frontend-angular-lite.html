About

Build your own simplified AngularJS in 200 lines: scopes, watchers, a digest loop, DI, directives and a module system — the complete magic of early Angular, unveiled.

What you'll learn

  • The digest loop: how Angular knew your data changed
  • $watch/$digest two-way binding mechanics
  • Dependency injection as a tiny registry
  • How directives map to DOM behavior

Core idea

The secret of the digest loop — the heartbeat of AngularJS:

    this.$$watchers.push(watcher);
