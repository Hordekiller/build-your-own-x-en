About

A Linux container runtime in 500 lines of C: namespaces, chroot and pivot_root to isolate a rootfs, hand-crafted hostnames, and an init process that babysits the container's first process.

What you'll learn

  • The syscalls behind containers: clone, unshare, chroot, pivot_root
  • Isolating processes with UTS, PID, mount and network namespaces
  • The init/socketpair handshake that guards the container's first process
  • Capabilities: dropping privileges and cleaning up the environment
