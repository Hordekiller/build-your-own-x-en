About

A deep series on building a rigid-body game physics engine: semi-implicit Euler integration, support functions and the GJK collision algorithm.

What you'll learn

  • Semi-implicit Euler: update velocities first, then positions
  • Support functions and the CSO (Minkowski difference)
  • The GJK algorithm: closest-point and overlap queries via the simplex

Core idea

Integration follows the semi-implicit pattern: velocities update from forces first, positions second. For collisions, the series uses support functions - the farthest point of a shape along a direction - and GJK, which grows a simplex in the Minkowski difference until it contains (or provably cannot contain) the origin.
