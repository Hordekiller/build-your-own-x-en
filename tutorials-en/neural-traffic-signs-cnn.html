About

Classifying German traffic signs with a convolutional network: preprocessing, a conv/maxpool feature pyramid, and a softmax head.

What you'll learn

  • Extracting the Y channel (YCbCr) and equalizing histograms
  • Convolution → ReLU → max-pooling stages at three scales
  • Feeding multi-scale features into a softmax classifier

Core idea

Images are preprocessed to grayscale luminance and histogram-equalized. The network then stacks three conv-pool stages, merges the multi-scale features, and ends in a softmax over sign classes.
