About

NES Emulator From Scratch: a video series that builds a full NES emulator live — the 6502 CPU, PPU graphics, cartridges, controllers and audio, one episode at a time.

What you'll learn

  • Emulating the 6502 instruction set
  • The PPU: nametables, sprites and palette handling
  • Header parsing: mapper 0 and cartridge basics
  • Running real NES games in your own emulator

Core idea

Every episode starts in the fetch unit — the opcode read that begins each cycle:

Why it matters

The 6502 and PPU combine into the most instructive emulator project on Earth, and this series shows every step — you emerge with a working NES and total understanding.
